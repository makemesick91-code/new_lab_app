## What shipped

config-driven, testable**. No migration, no route, no permission, no runtime
driver change, no queue worker enabled, no business workflow change, no
destructive command, KTP/NIK never exposed.

  patterns (config-not-code), final closure tag.

## Closure decision

`GO` — 16 completed roadmap entries, 13/13 closure criteria met, all 11 mandatory
ENT-5..15 gates GO, evidence/safety/CI wiring + operational scripts + runbooks
present, final `enterprise-foundation-go` tag declared, `next_recommended_sprint`
MON-1 (not stale), closure docs non-sensitive.

## Roadmap after ENT-16

ENT-16 closes the ENT enterprise foundation sequence. It is **not** the final
roadmap item — `MON-1` (Health Monitoring, Alerting & Uptime Readiness) is the
next planned sprint, followed by `PART-1` / `SEARCH-1` / `NDA-1` and the final
`RC-1` national consolidation. There is no ENT-17. On GO, the final
`enterprise-foundation-go` tag ends the initial Enterprise Foundation Freeze;
all later work inherits the ENT-5..16 rules.
